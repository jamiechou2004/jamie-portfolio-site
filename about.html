How I work

Curiosity, structure,
and attention to detail.

01

Ownership

I take responsibility for moving work from problem framing to design delivery, aligning with product and engineering and staying close to implementation details.

02

Working in ambiguity

I turn an open-ended brief into clear questions, distinguish what we know from what we assume, and use research and prototypes to make the next decision concrete.

03

Start with behavior

I look for the need behind a request, using research to question the first interpretation of a problem.

04

Learn by making

I use prototypes and small interaction experiments to explore timing, feedback, and how an idea feels in use.
