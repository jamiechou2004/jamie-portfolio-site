Context / Problem

Healthcare AI must explain its role before asking for trust.

Context

The team was shaping an AI-assisted healthcare web experience.

The work connected user onboarding, information clarity, client strategy, and the moments where AI guidance affects a sensitive decision.

Problem

Too much explanation overwhelms; too little makes the system feel unsafe.

Users need to understand what the AI can do, where its boundaries are, and when their own or a professional's judgment remains essential.

Constraint

Client confidentiality limits the public artifact.

This case preserves the project logic and Jamie's contribution while omitting client-specific screens, strategic details, and proprietary feedback.

Research

The research question was not “Do users trust AI?” but “What must they understand first?”

01 Synthesize client research

Organize evidence around first-use expectations, sensitive information, perceived system authority, and moments requiring reassurance.

02 Map information needs

Separate what users must know before they begin from what can be revealed progressively as the task becomes more specific.

03 Frame the client story

Connect trust needs to experience principles, an onboarding model, and a feasible product direction for stakeholder review.

Insights

Trust was shaped by hierarchy, boundaries, and recovery.

Insight 01

Onboarding carries trust

First-time users need a clear mental model before entering a sensitive AI-assisted flow.

Insight 02

AI guidance needs boundaries

The product should clarify what AI is helping with, what remains user-controlled, and when human judgment matters.

Insight 03

Recovery is part of credibility

A trustworthy experience needs a clear response when information is incomplete, confidence is low, or the AI cannot safely continue.

Design Decisions

Make accountability visible at the moments that carry risk.

01 / Expectations

State the AI's role before requesting sensitive input.

Introduce what the experience supports, what it does not replace, and how user information contributes to the result.

Creates a usable mental model
02 / Explanation

Reveal the “why” where it changes a decision.

Surface source context, uncertainty, and editable assumptions without turning every screen into a disclaimer.

Balances clarity and load
03 / Recovery

Design an explicit route out of uncertainty.

Use clarification, fallback, and human-support paths when the system cannot responsibly move the user forward.

Makes limits actionable

System / IA

Separate learning, input, recommendation, and next action.

01 Understand

Purpose, scope, privacy, and the role of AI appear before the task begins.

02 Contribute

Users provide information with context for why each input matters.

03 Review

AI output includes rationale, uncertainty, and editable assumptions.

04 Continue

Users confirm, clarify, recover, or move to human-supported action.

Execution

Trust is built as a sequence of understandable decisions.

01 Orient

State the purpose of the experience and set expectations before requesting sensitive input.

02 Explain

Show what the AI is considering, what information matters, and where uncertainty remains.

03 Confirm

Give users a deliberate review point with editable inputs before a recommendation becomes action.

04 Recover

Provide clarification, fallback, and human-support routes when the system cannot safely continue.

Testing and feedback

Feedback sharpened the balance between confidence and caution.

Feedback theme

Users need confidence, not certainty theater

The experience should avoid pretending AI is flawless. Trust improves when limitations are visible and recoverable.

Iteration

More guidance, less clutter

We refined the flow around staged explanations, clearer hierarchy, and more intentional moments for user confirmation.

Outcome

The concept aligned healthcare AI guidance with a clearer trust model.

The final direction gave the client team a structured onboarding and guidance narrative: establish expectations, explain consequential AI behavior, preserve user control, and provide recovery when confidence is limited.

Healthcare AI design has to earn user attention carefully.

Trust is designed through sequence
