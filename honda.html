HondaConnect / HMI + Companion App

A more connected
way to ride.

A Team Bravo concept exploring how the Honda Rebel 500 display and a companion app can support new riders before, during, and after a ride.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Research & UI design contribution

I primarily contributed to research and supported parts of the UI design. The work shown here is the team’s shared output.

PROJECT CONTEXT

Team Bravo · SCAD
SDES 495 + UXDG 315
Independent redesign concept

01 / Research

Designing around the rider’s attention.

The team’s research highlighted four needs: alignment with handlebar controls, glanceable information, simple navigation, and a clear hierarchy for notifications.

Research figures in this team presentation describe the shared study, not an individual contribution or a measured product outcome.

02 / Connected experience

One journey, two interfaces.

The companion app supports planning and rider connection. The motorcycle HMI brings ride information and updates into a compact round display.

03 / Interface direction

Keep trip information within reach.

The team explored a persistent trip information area across screens, so riders could access relevant information without switching to a separate trip page.

Independent student concept, not an official Honda product or partnership.
