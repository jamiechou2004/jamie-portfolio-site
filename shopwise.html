One order. Three stores. Three screens.

Shopwise turns the scattered job of furnishing a new place into one plan and one order, then carries it across iPhone, Apple Watch and a web console.

Problem → Value
Each piece of furniture lives in a different store, with its own cart, fees and delivery date, so nobody sees the whole room or the whole cost. Shopwise makes it one plan with one total, and runs the order behind the scenes.
Role
Product design and research, solo. Concept, IA, wireframes, hi-fi, three coded prototypes.
Status
Student concept. AI results, payments, stock and delivery are simulated.
