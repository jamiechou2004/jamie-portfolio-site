TL;DR

The design challenge was not adding AI. It was making AI feel useful fast.

For a consumer AI product, early product value depends on the user's first few decisions: what to ask, what to trust, what to do next, and why to come back. My work focused on turning AI capability into visible action paths and repeatable growth loops.

Context / Problem

A capable AI model still leaves a product question: where should the user begin?

Context

Capability was broad; user intent was not always formed.

Chance AI serves consumers who can arrive with different goals, levels of AI literacy, and expectations of what the product should do for them.

Problem

Blank or generic entry points shifted too much work to the user.

When the interface asked for a perfect prompt before demonstrating value, hesitation appeared before the product had earned attention or trust.

Constraint

The public story must protect active product work.

This case communicates the workflow logic and decision criteria. Sensitive screens, roadmap details, and performance metrics remain private.

Research

The work started by tracing where intent, trust, and momentum broke.

01 Review product signals

Read qualitative feedback and product behavior around first use, AI responses, value comprehension, and return intent.

02 Map the workflow

Trace the journey from entry and prompt formation through result interpretation, refinement, and a meaningful next action.

03 Audit growth moments

Evaluate where onboarding, feature discovery, and commercial prompts supported the task and where they interrupted it.

Insights

Three signals shaped the product direction.

Insight 01

Users need a reason to start

Contextual starters and workflow anchors reduce the cost of first action when users cannot yet form a precise prompt.

Insight 02

Trust depends on feedback

Users need to understand what the system is doing, why it is asking, and which assumptions or outputs they can change.

Insight 03

Growth needs a loop

Activation becomes more durable when a useful first result naturally leads to refinement, saving, or a reason to return.

Design Decisions

Turn open-ended capability into directed progress.

01 / Entry

Offer guided starts instead of an empty canvas.

Use contextual examples, templates, and sensible defaults that help users move before they know the perfect input.

Reduces first-action effort
02 / Feedback

Make system status and user control visible.

Clarify progress, intent, editable assumptions, and recovery paths so the AI feels responsive rather than opaque.

Builds interaction trust
03 / Growth

Place commercial UI after demonstrated usefulness.

Connect upgrade and feature discovery to moments when the product has made its value understandable.

Aligns growth with value

System / IA

One reusable structure connects onboarding, AI feedback, and return behavior.

01 Intent

Recognize the user's context and offer a relevant starting point.

02 Action

Guide input with examples, defaults, and low-friction decisions.

03 Feedback

Show progress, assumptions, output, and available controls.

04 Continuation

Make refine, save, share, upgrade, or return the logical next step.

Execution

The interaction loop: orient, act, refine, return.

01 Orient

Help users understand what the AI product can do in their current context.

02 Act

Reduce the number of decisions needed to create a useful first result.

03 Refine

Give users control over tone, direction, output, and next steps.

04 Return

Convert a successful first task into a repeatable habit or saved workflow.

Testing / Validation

Validate the sequence before polishing the surface.

01 Hypothesis

Guided starting points should reduce blank-state hesitation and help users reach value sooner.

02 Prototype

Test onboarding, AI feedback, refinement controls, and upgrade moments as one connected journey.

03 Observe

Look for uncertainty around system status, user control, value comprehension, and the next action.

04 Adjust

Reduce explanation, strengthen feedback, and place commercial prompts after demonstrated usefulness.

Outcome

The public outcome is a clearer activation direction, not an invented metric.

The product direction shifted from generic prompting toward guided user progress: clearer starts, more legible AI feedback, and growth surfaces tied to moments of demonstrated value.

Design signal
Clarity became part of the activation model.
